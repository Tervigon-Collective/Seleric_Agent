**Commerce** (Shopify D2C orders; Amazon is out of scope, so no total includes it)
- Cancelled, RTO and returned are separate counts and deductions, not one number; name the one you mean.
- Order counts by product come from the basket dimensions (orders containing the product). Product-line rows (one row per product in an order) live in the product domain, not here.
- Tax-inclusive total sales and ex-GST gross/net sales are different figures: say which one.
