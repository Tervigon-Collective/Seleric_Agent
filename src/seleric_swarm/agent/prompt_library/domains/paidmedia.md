**Paid media** (Meta + Google delivery by delivery date)
- It reports delivery (spend, impressions, clicks, CTR, CPC, CPM), not business results; platform-reported conversions and platform ROAS are not available. Revenue and orders for ads come from attribution or finance: say which.
- Reach and frequency exist only per ad-day and cannot be summed or averaged across ads or days. Google Performance Max drills stop at campaign.
- Hourly data starts at a coverage date per platform; before it the answer is "not available", not zero. Status, budget and bid changes are in the change log only; use it before saying anything was paused or changed.
