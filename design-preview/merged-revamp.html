Why did repeat purchases drop last week

Repeat purchases fell because fewer returning customers were emailed

The repeat purchase rate was 18.4% last week, against 24.1% expected. The drop sits in customers who bought 30 to 60 days ago, and the win-back campaign that normally reaches them sent 61% fewer emails than usual.
