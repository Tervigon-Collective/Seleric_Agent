# Open gaps and tasks — 2026-10-08

Audit of 182 live runs over 7 days. 66 of them hit a validation revision loop. Fixes are in gaurav 90fc6f5.

## Fixed in 90fc6f5
- **Comparison direction.** The executor now measures change from the earlier window to the later one. The live bug: "this month vs last month" shipped as `completed` with "net sales fell 696,778 → 767,584".
- **Period-to-date comparisons** (this week, this month). The earlier window is cut to the same span, with its last day stopping at the elapsed hour (`services/elapsed.same_span`). The scope-coverage gate uses the same rule.
- **Metrics with no hourly series** (such as net_profit) fall back to whole days and say so.
- **Grounding gate.** A figure taken from a finding now counts as showing its source metric. Gap messages list the values the evidence holds.
- **Revision loop:**
  - Mis-copied citation ids are repaired without a revision.
  - Metric ids that have no label are shown as plain words.
  - A revision with the same figures and the same rejection stops (`VALIDATION_STALLED`).
  - A total the mission fetched is not treated as a table mismatch.
  - When revisions are exhausted, the answer ships without the irreconcilable total instead of failing.

## Open gaps
1. **gpt-5-mini rate limits (429).** understand and the agent share one TPM quota, and 429s pushed runs to 196–329 s. Needs a quota increase or a separate deployment for understand.
2. **Scope gate on "is X responsible?" questions.** A value named as a candidate cause (e.g. "Meta") is required as a filter on all evidence. Diagnosis needs both the total and the slice. This gate drove 29 rejections.
3. **Unbacked figures (31 rejections).** The model computes numbers itself. It should derive them with `analyze` or `run_python` so they are recorded.
4. **No premise check or decomposition before blame.** "Sales haven't been great" is never verified against the total. The agent is a pipeline: understand → plan → prefetch → one answer, with gates. It needs a hypothesis loop: check the premise, decompose the change, then drill down.
5. **Q17 "other sources".** This resolves to payment_method=other; no model marks it as an ordinary word.
6. **Insight "Worth a look" noise.** Hourly-snapshot risk signals can contradict the answer's window, e.g. a repeat-customer count compared across windows of unequal length.
7. **Uncommitted WIP in the live checkout** hardcodes metric names: `_DELIVERY_METRICS` and `_SPEND_SLICE_COMPANIONS` (catalogue_bootstrap), and `_MERGE_DERIVED` (analytics). Its ROAS formula disagrees with the catalogue's. It also auto-cites all mission evidence, which triggers the "shows no value" loop. Keep the rule: no hardcoded metric names, keywords or regex.
